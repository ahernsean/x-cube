X-Cube Lab

3D view

Swipe on the cube to turn a layer. Drag the background, or long press the cube and drag, to tumble the view. Two fingers spin it; double tap undoes.

During a solve step, only the cubelets the step applies to keep their colors; gray ones don't move, dark gray ones may end up anywhere. Each colored cubelet has a path from one of its stickers, in that sticker's color, to where that sticker will be, and a small copy of the cubelet flies along it. With a ring: the cubelet ends up solved. Dashed: it is passing through and moves again later. Plain: a shape or core step moves it; it is solved later.

Pick a color, then tap stickers on the cube to paint them. Drag to turn the view.
